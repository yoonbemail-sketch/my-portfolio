## Question

“배치” means **where to send a free car while it waits**, not shaft construction. In apartments, callers above floor 1 usually want the lobby; evening arrivals usually leave from the lobby to a home floor. Lobby-only parking can help ingress and hurt egress — and the reverse for stay/spread.

## Traffic model (apartment default)

| Period | Typical flow |
| --- | --- |
| Morning egress | Upper floors → lobby |
| Evening ingress | Lobby → upper floors |
| Midday / off-peak | Both directions, lower rate shape |

Interfloor trips (upper↔upper) default to **10%** and are adjustable. Office any-floor OD is deferred as a later building-type switch.

## What you can compare

| Control | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Parking strategy | Stay / Lobby / Mid / Spread / Demand |
| Traffic period | Morning / Evening / Midday |
| Interfloor % | Share of non-lobby residential trips |
| Door dwell | Stop time at each floor |
| Building knobs | Floors, cars, capacity, arrival rate |

## Metrics

- **Avg / Max wait** — hall call to board
- **Completed** — trips finished vs target
- **Empty travel** — floors moved with no passengers (parking cost)

## How it runs

Each tick: spawn apartment OD passengers → assign nearest capable car → move / door dwell / board-alight → if idle, apply the selected parking policy.
