## Question

Elevator “배치” here means **where to send a free car while it waits**, not shaft construction. Under up-peak (lobby → upper floors), lobby parking often cuts wait; under down-peak the same policy wastes empty travel and leaves upper halls uncovered.

## What you can compare

| Control | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Parking strategy | Stay / Lobby / Mid / Spread / Demand |
| Peak mode | Mixed / Up-peak / Down-peak / Lunch |
| Building knobs | Floors, car count, capacity, arrival rate |

## Metrics

- **Avg / Max wait** — hall call to board
- **Completed** — trips finished vs target
- **Empty travel** — floors moved with no passengers (parking cost)

## How it runs

Each tick: spawn OD passengers by peak mode → assign nearest capable car → move / door dwell / board-alight → if idle, apply the selected parking policy. UI follows the boarding-sim pattern (strategy dropdown, sliders, Start/Pause/Reset, live dashboard).
